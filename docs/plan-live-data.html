Make live delays match the station

RB89 Geseke 10:25 was +7 on the platform, in DB Navigator, and in Padersprinter. Our app showed it on time.

Short version

Every provider had the delay. Our pipeline lost it in three places.

Fix: build the live frame from departure boards per leg instead of one VRR trip search, keep plan times purely scheduled, and stop the app from erasing a delay it cannot re-check.

Starting point

Live data passes through four layers, and the last one can erase it

refreshVisible in AppViewModel stores the SRS result even when verified is false, and TripResultSet.overlay copies its empty expected times over the backend's.

Design

One board lookup per leg end, cached and shared

A leg is a departure at one stop and an arrival at another, which is exactly what a departure board keys on: planned minute plus line. That works for every itinerary, not only the three VRR picks. VRR stays first because its bus data agreed with the operator where Transitous did not.

backend
parse_transitous takes itinerary dep and arr from the first and last leg's scheduled times. New upstream::slot_board (DM with itdTime, dep or arr) and plan::live from boards. vrr_live goes.
app
RouteRealtime keeps a leg's incoming forecast when it cannot match it. BackendApi.toRoutes marks cancelled legs. The SRS re-check stays as a second opinion inside Kreis Paderborn.
Risks

More VRR calls is the main cost

Cap of 8 per frame. Boards are shared across users and with /watch, so a warm cache costs 0.

VRR load
Cap 8 lookups, 20 s cache, skip legs more than 90 min out. They have no forecast yet anyway.
line names
RB 89 vs RB89 is handled by norm_line. Replacement buses (SEV RE11) will not match and stay on schedule.
arr guess
Fallback 3 assumes a delay holds to the next stop. Used only when the DM arrival board has no estimate.

Open decisions

Three choices for you

horizon
Live lookups for legs up to 90 min ahead. Longer costs more calls for little gain.
SRS ids
Map DELFI ids to SRS ids (VRR properties.stopId, cached) so the SRS re-check works outside Kreis Paderborn. I would skip it for now. P3 alone fixes the symptom.
cancelled
Show a cancelled connection crossed out, or hide it like gone does today. I would show it.
